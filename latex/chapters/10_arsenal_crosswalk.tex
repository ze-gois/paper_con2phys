\section{Theorems without a dedicated implementation}
The catalogue also includes Kolmogorov extension, time-rescaling
goodness-of-fit, Gaussian KL theory, Davis--Kahan perturbation,
Takens embedding, and Kalman observability. These are
\emph{mathematical prerequisites or future checks}, not claims
that their complete scientific implementations exist in the
repository. The presence of \texttt{\detokenize{gaussian_process_smoothing.m}}
must not be interpreted as a full GPFA model.

\section{Operational contract}
Read input shapes and units from the MATLAB function help.
Document sample rate, time stamps, channel orientation,
boundary conventions, electrode reference, trial alignment,
and the meaning of each null hypothesis. Benchmark a method
against simulated data of known ground truth before trying
to infer physiology from an empirical recording.
